## Summary
1. The Hooded Figure's eligibility rule works for several human GMs but not for one: a solo GM is counted as "the loser" every season, even after winning the Super Bowl (seed 21: champion in 2027, then offered the deal after one 5-12 year).
2. Targeting is intentional: negative outcomes hit a CPU division rival; positive ones boost your roster. The screens are honest but anonymous; you are never told which news is yours.
3. Payment value is strongly convex ($10M always tier 4, whole CPU roster out for the season) and low payments rarely touch you.
4. Two small correctness issues (preseason week overlap, no idempotence guard) and one information leak (`unavailable` in the redacted league document).
5. No enjoyment scores were given: I did not play the game as a human would. Presentation findings are limited to what static screens show.

## Findings

### Finding A5-001 — One-human league: every season counts as a "losing" one, including a title year
- Class: bug (suspected; spec not in repo)
- Suggested severity: High
- Mode: SP
- Screen/system: `state/hoodedFigure.ts` `determineHoodedFigureLosers` / `updateHoodedFigureStreaks`; stage `hoodedFigureEncounter`
- Grounding: played-in-UI (real-engine playthrough) + controlled-sim
- Observed: seed 21, GB: 13-4 and Super Bowl champions in 2027 → streak 1; 5-12 in 2028 → streak 2 → offered the figure ("Your team is struggling"). Offered in 2028, 2029, 2030 (3 of 3 eligible offseasons). `determineHoodedFigureLosers([15-2 champion])` → `["GB"]`. On a synthetic league, a contender solo GM is eligible 10 per 10 seasons under the current rule; 702 of those offers followed a title.
- Expected: `CLAUDE.md` and `decisions.md` say two *losing* seasons. A champion is not a losing franchise.
- Repro: 1) `A5_*` playthrough harness (REPRODUCE.md) or call `determineHoodedFigureLosers` with one outcome (a champion). Reproducibility: always.
- Evidence: `evidence/run-seed21-GB-REAL-ENGINE.json`, `hf-montecarlo-1800.json` (`soloChampLoser`), `e1_eligibility_rules.json` (4,000 seasons/cell)
- Root cause: confirmed for the mechanism (only human teams are compared); the intended solo behaviour is unknown.
- Proposed solution: require the relative loser to also have missed the playoffs ("rule D"). Solo: 3.3 offers/10 seasons (average team), 0.24 (contender), 8.6 (poor). Multi: unchanged selection, 1.9 (2 avg), 0.9 (4 avg).
- Alternatives: B (below .500); add a one-season cooldown (poor team 8.6 → 4.5).
- Difficulty: S
- Expected benefit: restores rarity, fixes the copy for contenders
- Related: A5-008; Agent 3 (competitiveness), Agent 8 (multiplayer)
- Regression check: unit cases for one human: champion → none, wild-card exit → none, 6-11 miss → loser; two-human tests unchanged
- Uncertainty: spec §4 unread; real intent for solo unknown; synthetic league has fixed team strength

### Finding A5-003 — Low payments rarely change the payer's season
- Class: balance · Severity: Medium · Mode: both · System: `resolveNegativeBranch`, `applyPositionGroupBoost`
- Grounding: controlled-sim (3,000 resolutions, seed 77)
- Observed: at $3M a negative outcome makes the target miss a game against the payer 16% of the time (9% at $1M, 69% at $10M); 21% of $3M offers produce nothing felt. A division rival is 2 of 17 weeks, so a one-week absence lands about 12% of the time. In the natural run: CHI/DET punter or starters out for 1 game while GB went 3-14 and 4-13.
- Expected: a payment visibly matters, or the player knows it often will not.
- Repro: `a5_payment_ev_E4` harness; always.
- Evidence: `e4_payment_ev_raw.json`
- Root cause: by design (target is a CPU rival; one-week durations).
- Proposed solution: let Agent 3 measure win-probability per event; candidate: schedule-aware short absences.
- Alternatives: say so in the terms line (A5-002). Difficulty: M. Benefit: payments feel meaningful.
- Regression check: simulation measuring payer and rival win% by payment over ≥ 300 seasons
- Uncertainty: no in-engine win-probability measured; fixture schedule is the mock slate

### Finding A5-005 — Value is convex in payment; $10M always buys tier 4
- Class: balance · Severity: Medium · Mode: both
- Grounding: controlled-sim; code-inspected
- Observed: $10M → 100% tier 4 (300/300). $3M → $10M: cost ×3.3, felt-hit chance ×4.3, boost ×3.6. "Pay the maximum your cap allows" dominates.
- Expected: the hidden-threshold gamble should matter across the range.
- Root cause: confirmed: thresholds clamped to ≤ 9.9 and `MAX_PAYMENT = 10`; the clamp looks deliberate.
- Proposed solution: only if you want a gamble at the top: let the top threshold sit above the cap. Evidence for any change should come from Agent 3.
- Difficulty: S. Related: A5-018. Regression check: tier distribution at $10M.
- Uncertainty: intended behaviour not documented

### Finding A5-014 — Redacted league still carries `hoodedFigure.unavailable`
- Class: security (information leak, low severity) · Severity: Medium · Mode: MP

- Grounding: test-reproduced
- Observed: with two humans, after BUF's negative bargain, KC's redacted copy has no BUF encounter but still lists the sabotaged CPU player (e.g. a MIA player) in `unavailable` before the reveal. 8 of the first 12 sampled negative outcomes exposed ≥ 1 id (38 trials).
