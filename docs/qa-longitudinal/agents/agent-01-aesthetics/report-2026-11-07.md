## Summary (≤5 lines)
Two consecutive seasons plus the 2029 offseason were played through the real browser against the real engine, and every screen on the list was rendered except a few listed below. The design is strong where it was converted (roster, Gamecast, bracket, hub, Hooded Figure) but eight "decision" screens and every draft/FA/trade summary still run on the old dark skin, and the stat strip on them is unreadable (measured 1.02:1). **New and most serious: after about 2.1 seasons the save (5.16 M characters) hits the browser's ~5.2 M-character localStorage limit and the dynasty silently stops saving.** Root causes for the main visual findings are now confirmed in code; none are covered by the Group 08 "pixel-identical" cleanup and need a separate small PR first.

### Finding A1-023 — The dynasty silently stops saving partway through season 2
- Class: bug · Suggested severity: **High** · Mode: SP · Screen/system: `ui-source/src/state/store.ts` (persist → single `localStorage` key `nfl-sim-ui.league`), banner in `components/AppShell.tsx:329`
- Grounding: played-in-UI + measured
- Observed: Saved league string is **5,163,796 chars at 2028 week 15** (about 2.4 MB after one season: fixture end-of-season saves are 2.7 MB). Chromium accepted at most **5,234,375 chars** in a single key on the same origin. In the real run the banner "This dynasty isn't being saved. The browser refused to write to storage … closing this tab will lose everything since the last successful save" was showing on the 2028 Season Complete screen (the first time I looked after week 15); reloading from the last stored state returned the league to 2028 week 15.
- Expected: A multi-season dynasty (the product's premise) should persist without a ~2-season ceiling.
- Repro: 1) New dynasty, any team. 2) Play 2027 and 2028 through week ~15 with the real adapter. 3) Watch for the red notice; reload the tab. Reproducibility: always in this build/browser (2 of 2 runs).
- Evidence: `evidence/wave2/save_failure_banner_2028_eos.webp`; `tools/quota.cjs` (prints both numbers); `evidence/wave2/real-run-log-2027-2029.txt`.
- Root cause: confirmed — save grows with each season (history, stats, box scores) while localStorage allows ~5 M chars per origin. The code's own comment (store.ts ~1583) says "A league is ~2.5MB and a browser gives a site about 5MB" and shows the warning, i.e. the limit is known; the notice says "full quota, or a private window" without telling the player what to do.
- Proposed solution: store the league in IndexedDB (async, hundreds of MB) with localStorage as a tiny pointer; or compress (`CompressionStream`) and prune per-season detail older than N seasons into a compact history; make the warning actionable ("Download backup now" button, which already exists).
- Alternatives: automatic "Download backup" prompt at 80% of quota; cap history detail.
- Difficulty: M–L · Expected benefit: no lost dynasties; the "multi-year dynasty" promise holds.
- Related: A4 (gaps), A7; `SaveBackup.tsx`; online leagues are server-saved and unaffected.
- Regression check: simulate 5 seasons headless and assert save size stays under a stated budget / IndexedDB round-trip; reload after season 3 restores the stage.
- Uncertainty: Firefox/Safari limits not measured; the online copy of a save (`ONLINE_SAVE_KEY`) can double usage; I did not test whether Download backup still works at this size.

### Finding A1-024 — 28 native `confirm()` / `alert()` calls sit outside the design system
- Class: UX · **Medium** · Mode: both · Screen/system: ReadinessGate, RookieDraftSummary/FantasyDraftSummary, HoodedFigureEncounter, TradeProposal, OnlineLobby, Checkpoint, ExpiringContracts, SaveBackup, AppShell, CommissionerTakeTurn, UniversePicker (28 call sites by `grep`)
- Grounding: played-in-UI (seen: "Continue to Rookie Signings? You can't return to this summary.", "Pay the figure $3.5M of cap space? There's no undo.", an `alert()` "Your staff wouldn't extend anyone…" fired repeatedly from "Let my staff re-sign the core") + code-inspected (the rest)
- Observed: Irreversible, high-stakes choices (advance a stage, accept a trade, pay the Hooded Figure, archive a league, make someone commissioner) use the browser's grey dialog; the rest of the app uses gameday-styled dialogs ("Draft X?", "Make the offer" with "No thanks"). Automation and some mobile browsers suppress/decline native dialogs, which made "Advance" a silent no-op in my first runs.
- Expected: one dialog system; consequences stated in the design's voice; buttons named for the action.
- Repro: Rookie Draft Summary → "Continue to Rookie Signings". Reproducibility: always.
- Root cause: confirmed — direct `confirm()` use; an in-app `ConfirmDecision` and `FullScreenOverlay` already exist.
- Proposed solution: a shared `useConfirm()` hook rendering the existing modal-card; replace sites in priority: trade accept, advance-with-no-return, Hooded Figure pay, destructive league actions.
- Difficulty: M · Benefit: consistent, readable, testable consequences. Related: A1-032.
- Regression check: e2e specs that currently accept dialogs via `page.on('dialog')` need updating; keyboard/focus trap tests.
- Uncertainty: native dialogs in real mobile Safari/Chrome not observed.

### Finding A1-029 — Several contrast failures depend on the franchise colour
- Class: accessibility · **Medium** · Mode: SP

### Finding A1-031 — Gamecast: quarter-break modal halts autoplay and freezes stepping; otherwise excellent
- Class: UX · **Low–Medium** · Mode: both · `screens/gamecast/*`
- Grounding: played-in-UI (real-engine adapter game, `2028-PRE-1-CIN-BAL`, 149 plays)
- Observed: at 1× the viewer advances one play every ~1.4 s (counter 1→10 in 10.3 s), ≈3.5 minutes for a game; at each quarter a "NOW STARTING QUARTER 2 — Continue" modal appears, the controls grey out and the play counter does not move until Continue is pressed (30 "next step" clicks after the modal left the counter at 39). The modal's Continue is yellow while every other primary is orange. On phones the Methodology button covers the key-moments card and the field becomes a dark panel when the modal is open.
- Expected: autoplay should continue after a short beat; one primary colour.
- Proposed: auto-dismiss the quarter card after ~1.2 s when autoplay is on (reduced-motion: static); keep Continue for stepping mode; use the team accent only for the score blocks.
- Difficulty: S · Benefit: hands-off watching works.
- Regression: Gamecast playback tests; reduced-motion.
- Uncertainty: the "1×" speed button cycles; I did not verify 0.5×/2× pace. Positive: see below.
